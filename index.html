ABOUT BOOKIT

A Modern Venue Platform for College Facilities

BookIt was built to replace outdated physical register logs, eliminate scheduling collisions, and digitize venue permissions for students, faculty, and administrative departments.

Whether organizing an international symposium in the Main Auditorium, booking New Classroom 28 for student workshops, or conducting a coding marathon in Cognizant Lab, BookIt gives instant slot-by-slot transparency modeled after Indian Railways’ robust reservation systems.
