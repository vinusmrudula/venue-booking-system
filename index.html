ABOUT BOOKIT

A Modern Venue Platform for College Facilities

BookIt was built to replace outdated physical register logs, eliminate scheduling collisions, and digitize venue permissions for students, faculty, and administrative departments.

Whether organizing an international symposium in the Main Auditorium, booking NC-27 for student workshops, or reserving Academics Complex halls, BookIt gives instant slot-by-slot transparency modeled after Indian Railways’ robust reservation systems.
